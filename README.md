# 🌬️ AirNova – The Smart Air Purifier  

✨ **AirNova** is a compact, eco-friendly, and innovative air purification system designed by a team from **General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University**.  
Its mission goes beyond technology – it’s about delivering **cleaner, healthier air** and uplifting the lives of **underprivileged families** exposed to harmful cooking smoke.  

---

## 🔹 Features  
✅ Detects harmful smoke using **Arduino Nano + MQ-135 sensor**  
✅ Purifies air with **multi-layer activated carbon filters**  
✅ Eliminates germs using **UV light sterilization**  
✅ Ensures airflow with a **powerful exhaust fan**  
✅ Works in a **3D-printed, space-saving design**, perfect for single-room homes  
✅ **Automatic operation**:  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Switches **on** when smoke exceeds threshold  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Switches **off** when air is clean  
✅ Operates **quietly** with an **energy-efficient design**  

---

## 💡 Our Mission  
To reduce **respiratory health risks**, minimize exposure to **cooking smoke**, and create **equitable access to clean air** for everyone.  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack  
- **Arduino Nano**  
- **MQ-135 Gas Sensor**  
- **UV Light Module**  
- **Multi-layer Carbon Filters**  
- **3D Printed Housing**  
- **Exhaust Fan**  

---

## 🎯 Impact  
AirNova was built with the vision of combining **innovation, sustainability, and social good**.  
It provides an **affordable, space-conscious solution** to improve air quality in underprivileged households.
